Privacy Policy

Nityam (नित्यम)

Effective Date: October 2026 | Last Updated: November 2026

Welcome to Nityam ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal data remains safe. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, discloses, and protects your information.

🔒 Core Principle: Nityam is built with a local-first privacy philosophy. Your daily habits, streaks, reflections, and notes belong to you and are stored locally on your device by default.

1. Information We Collect

We collect and handle minimal data necessary to provide a smooth, secure habit-tracking experience:

  • Local App Data: Habits, completion timestamps, monthly streaks, custom categories, sacred verses preferences, and personal smart notes. This data is stored strictly in the local Room Database on your device.
  • Account Information (Optional): If you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In or Firebase Authentication, we collect your name, email address, and profile picture provided by your authentication provider to sync your account details.
  • Device & Diagnostics Information: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic information (such as crash logs via Firebase Crashlytics, device model, and OS version) to identify and fix technical issues.
  • Advertising & Identifiers (Google AdMob): We use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display banner, native, and interstitial advertisements. AdMob may automatically collect non-sensitive device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / AAID) to serve relevant, policy-compliant ads and prevent ad fraud.

2. Biometric Data & Security

Nityam offers an optional App Lock feature using Android's official BiometricPrompt API (Fingerprint / Face Unlock / Device PIN):

  • The app never collects, reads, stores, or transmits your fingerprint or facial biometric data.
  • Authentication is handled entirely by the secure Android OS Keystore / Hardware Enclave on your device. The app only receives a cryptographic success or failure callback.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To enable habit tracking, streak calculations, and progress analytics.
  • To display daily spiritual verses (Sacred Verses) and support bilingual (Hindi & English) features.
  • To send scheduled local daily habit reminders and notification alarms (if enabled by you).
  • To display privacy-safe advertisements via Google AdMob to support the continuous development of the application.
  • To provide customer support and troubleshoot bugs when you reach out to us.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party or advertising brokers.

We only use trusted, privacy-compliant service providers:

  • Google Play Services: Core Android system functionality and in-app updates.
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): Used to serve banner, native, and interstitial ads. AdMob processes device identifiers and interaction telemetry in accordance with Google's Advertising Policies. You can manage or reset your Advertising ID via Android Settings > Google > Ads. Learn more at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • Firebase Authentication & Crashlytics (Google LLC): Secure sign-in management and anonymous crash triage. You can read Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Users have complete control over their personal data:

  • Local Data: You can clear your data at any time via App Settings, or by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings.
  • In-App Account Deletion: If you use an online account, you can permanently delete your account, synced profile data, and cloud records directly inside the app under Profile > Delete account.
  • Web-Based Deletion Request: If you wish to request account and data deletion outside the app, you can email developer support directly at suyashpan262@gmail.com with your registered email address, and your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

Nityam does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

7. Permissions Requested

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on Android 13+ to send your scheduled habit reminders.
  • USE_BIOMETRIC: Required to unlock the app when you turn on the App Auto-Lock feature.
  • INTERNET: Required for optional Firebase sync, crash logging, and external link navigation.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be posted here with an updated "Last Updated" date.
